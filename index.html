Know in 30

Know in 30 is a small, personally run set of YouTube channels publishing short educational videos: general-knowledge explainers, cricket statistics and Tamil cinema box-office numbers.

This site exists as the home page of the "Know in 30" tool, a private publishing utility used only by its owner to upload videos to, and read statistics from, his own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
